Craft & Heritage

Hours of Handwork
in Every Lapel

Our artisans are fifth and sixth-generation masters of Aari embroidery and Chikankari — techniques that have been passed down through families for centuries across the communities of India. Each stitch carries the weight of cultural memory.

At Arth Atelier, we don't just preserve these traditions. We place them at the center of contemporary fashion, creating pieces where heritage becomes a living, wearable thing.

Traceability

From Farm to Fiber
to Your Wardrobe

Every Arth Atelier garment comes with a QR code that traces its journey — from the farm where the fiber was harvested, to the spinning, weaving, dyeing, and hand-finishing. Complete transparency, no exceptions.

We work exclusively with natural, organic, and recycled materials: traceable organic wool, peace silk, Lyocell, and upcycled deadstock fabrics. Our supply chain is short, sustainable, and intentional.
