The Cardigan,
Reimagined

What happens when fifth-generation embroidery masters apply their craft to the most familiar knit silhouette? Something entirely new. Every motif on this cardigan is drawn from centuries of Kashmiri pattern-making — then placed with a modern eye that balances negative space against intricate detail.

The organic wool is sourced from traceable farms and spun into a weight that drapes, never clings. It is outerwear as art object.

The Process

From Farm to Fiber
to Your Wardrobe

Every garment is traceable from raw fiber to finished piece. We work exclusively with organic wool, peace silk, Lyocell, and upcycled deadstock — each material chosen for both its beauty and its impact.
