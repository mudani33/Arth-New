The Collection

East Meets
West

Every piece in this collection begins with a conversation between heritage and modernity. Fifth-generation artisans apply centuries-old Aari embroidery and Chikankari techniques to silhouettes drawn from contemporary menswear — blazers, cardigans, trousers cut for the way we live now.

The result is clothing that carries the weight of tradition without the burden of costume. Garments that belong in a boardroom, on a terrace, or walking the streets of any city in the world.

Alibaug, India

Where Land
Meets Sea

Alibaug's coastal light and weathered architecture provided the perfect backdrop — a landscape shaped by time, much like the craft traditions that define each Arth Atelier garment. Every piece was shot in natural light, without retouching, to honor both fabric and form.

The Craft

Made
by Hand

Each garment requires dozens of hours of hand embroidery — stitches placed one at a time by artisans who have inherited these techniques through five and six generations. There is no shortcut, no machine equivalent. Only patience, mastery, and the kind of beauty that comes from human touch.

We work exclusively with organic wool, peace silk, Lyocell, and upcycled deadstock. Every material is traceable from farm to finished piece.
