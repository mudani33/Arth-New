Our Brand

Where Heritage
Meets the Hand

Designed in New England. Handcrafted in India.

Arth

From the Sanskrit for meaning or significance — the intention behind every thread.

Atelier

An artist's workshop — a space where things are made slowly, and by hand.

Arth Atelier is a contemporary, ready-to-wear brand blending artisanal craftsmanship with modern design. Rooted in an East-meets-West philosophy, every piece is a celebration of craft, heritage, and meticulous workmanship.

Born from a deep desire to create garments that carry emotional and cultural depth — pieces that feel personal, rooted, yet global — the brand exists to highlight generations of artists who have passed down traditional techniques that are a cherished part of India's rich cultural heritage.

That reverence for the handmade — for the spinners, knitters, and embroiderers whose artistry and work often goes unseen — is what drives every collection.

East Meets West

At the heart of Arth Atelier is a design ideology that merges East and West into a single, fluid narrative — the precision of Western tailoring met with the soul and beauty of Eastern craft.

Minimalism and adornment coexist. Structure and drape find harmony. Traditional embroidery techniques are reinterpreted on clean, contemporary silhouettes. The result is clothing that feels timeless yet distinctly of today.

"Where I'm from, the way of living is slow and intentional. Clothing always meant buying fabric and getting it tailored."

— Tamanna Mullen, Founder

The Artisans

Many of the kaarigars we work with are fifth or sixth generation masters of techniques that are both endangered and irreplaceable. Every piece has some form of handwork — proudly featuring traditional techniques that exist across different communities of India.

We collaborate closely with small artisan clusters, building relationships based on respect and the creative process — until the craft feels honored, wearable, and effortless on the body.
