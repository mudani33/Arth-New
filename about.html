Our Brand

Where Heritage
Meets the Hand

Designed in New England. Handcrafted in India.

Arth

From the Sanskrit for meaning or significance — the intention behind every thread.

Atelier

An artist's workshop — a space where things are made slowly, and by hand.

A contemporary, ready-to-wear brand blending artisanal craftsmanship with modern design. Rooted in an East-meets-West philosophy, every piece is a celebration of craft, heritage, and meticulous workmanship.

"Where I'm from, the way of living is slow and intentional. Clothing always meant buying fabric and getting it tailored."

01

The Art

Heritage patterns meet contemporary silhouettes — an East-meets-West sensibility that honors tradition while speaking to the modern wardrobe.

02

The Craft

Aari embroidery, Chikankari, hand-weaving — executed by fifth and sixth generation kaarigars whose mastery is both endangered and irreplaceable.

03

The Impact

A short, traceable supply chain built on natural and organic materials, fair partnerships, and full transparency from farm to finished garment.

The Artisans

Many of the kaarigars we work with are fifth or sixth generation masters of techniques that are both endangered and irreplaceable. We collaborate closely, building relationships based on respect — until the craft feels honored, wearable, and effortless on the body.

"We treat suppliers as partners rather than vendors. That collaboration has uncovered innovative pathways that larger, faster systems often overlook."

— Tamanna Mullen
