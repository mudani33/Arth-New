Atelier

An artist's workshop — a space where things are made slowly, and by hand.

The Brand

A contemporary, ready-to-wear brand blending artisanal craftsmanship with high fashion and modern design. Rooted in an East-meets-West philosophy, every piece is a celebration of craft, heritage, and meticulous workmanship.

Born from a deep desire to create garments that carry emotional and cultural depth — pieces that feel personal, and rooted, yet global — the brand exists to highlight generations of artists who have passed down traditional techniques that are a cherished part of India's rich cultural heritage.

"Where I'm from, the way of living is slow and intentional. Clothing always meant buying fabric and getting it tailored."

That reverence for the handmade — for the spinners, knitters, and embroiderers whose artistry and work often goes unseen — is what drives every collection.

Arth Atelier exists to highlight and bring to the world the work of generations of artists who have passed down traditional techniques that are a cherished part of India's rich cultural heritage.

Design Philosophy

A Dialogue Between
Cultural Expressions

At the heart of Arth Atelier is a design ideology that merges East and West into a single, fluid narrative — the precision of Western tailoring met with the soul and beauty of Eastern craft.

Within this dialogue, minimalism and adornment coexist, structure and drape find harmony, and traditional embroidery techniques are reinterpreted on clean, contemporary silhouettes. The result is clothing that feels timeless yet distinctly of today.

Our Foundation

Three Pillars

Every decision has been guided by a value system built on three interconnected principles.

I

The Art

The creative vision — where heritage patterns, contemporary silhouettes, and an East-meets-West sensibility converge into designs that honor tradition while speaking to the modern wardrobe.

II

The Craft

The human hand — Aari embroidery, Chikankari, hand-weaving, and block printing executed by fifth and sixth generation kaarigars whose mastery is both endangered and irreplaceable.

III

The Impact

The responsibility — a short, traceable supply chain built on natural and organic materials, fair partnerships, and full transparency from farm to finished garment.

The Artisans

Generations of Mastery

Many of the kaarigars we work with are fifth or sixth generation masters of techniques that are both endangered and irreplaceable. Every piece at Arth Atelier has some form of handwork to it — proudly featuring and experimenting with traditional techniques that exist across different communities of India.

We collaborate closely with small artisan clusters, building relationships based on respect and the creative process. Techniques like Aari are chosen not only for their beauty and precision, but for how their rhythm, scale, and texture can live on an oversized blazer, a fluid trouser, or a merino cardigan — without overwhelming the silhouette.

From there, the process becomes a collaboration — sampling, re-scaling, and sometimes reducing motifs — until the craft feels honored, wearable, and effortless on the body.

"We treat suppliers as partners rather than vendors. That collaboration has uncovered innovative pathways that larger, faster systems often overlook."

— Tamanna Mullen
