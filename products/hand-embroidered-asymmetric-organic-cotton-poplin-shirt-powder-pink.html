Asymmetric Organic Cotton Poplin Shirt — Powder Pink

$495

Our architectural asymmetric shirt in a soft powder pink. Cut from organic cotton poplin with an off-centre placket and delicate hand-embroidery by our artisan partners in Lucknow, India. The gentle pink hue adds a touch of warmth to the contemporary silhouette — a piece that moves effortlessly from day to evening.

Materials & Craft

100% GOTS-certified organic cotton poplin. Hand-embroidered using traditional Aari technique by multi-generational artisan families in Lucknow, India. Each shirt requires approximately 20 hours of delicate handwork.

Mother-of-pearl buttons on asymmetric placket. French seam construction throughout for a clean interior finish.

Size & Fit

Relaxed contemporary fit with an asymmetric front closure. Slightly oversized through the body with a curved hem. We recommend ordering your usual size for an effortless drape.

Care

Machine wash cold on gentle cycle or hand wash. Hang to dry. Iron on low heat on reverse side to protect embroidery. Avoid bleach. Wash separately on first use to prevent color transfer.

Shipping & Returns

Complimentary shipping on all orders. Made-to-order pieces ship within 4–6 weeks. Returns accepted within 14 days of delivery for unworn items in original condition.
