Hand-Embroidered Single-Breasted Wool Blazer

$995

A refined single-breasted blazer featuring elegant outline hand-embroidery by our artisan partners in Lucknow, India — families who have practiced Aari embroidery for generations. Cut from Italian-milled merino wool with a clean, linear embroidery pattern that brings understated sophistication to a contemporary silhouette.

Materials & Craft

100% Italian-milled merino wool. Hand-embroidered using traditional Aari technique by multi-generational artisan families in Lucknow, India. This version features a refined outline pattern — clean linear embroidery that is less dense than the Luxe edition, requiring approximately 40 hours of meticulous handwork.

Mother-of-pearl buttons. Fully lined in cupro for a luxurious hand feel.

Size & Fit

Relaxed contemporary fit. Single-breasted, two-button closure. Notch lapels. Slightly dropped shoulder for an effortless silhouette. We recommend ordering your usual size.

Care

Professional dry clean only. Store on a padded hanger in a breathable garment bag. Avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight to preserve embroidery color.

Shipping & Returns

Complimentary shipping on all orders. Made-to-order pieces ship within 4–6 weeks. Returns accepted within 14 days of delivery for unworn items in original condition.

The Craft

Refined Lines,
Timeless Craft

The Wool Blazer features a refined outline embroidery — a clean, linear interpretation of the Aari technique that lets the beauty of the Italian merino wool shine through. Where the Luxe edition offers dense, immersive pattern work, this blazer takes a quieter approach with elegant restraint.

Every stitch is placed by hand by our artisan partners in Lucknow, requiring approximately 40 hours of focused handwork. The result is a blazer that bridges contemporary Western tailoring with centuries-old Indian craft tradition — understated enough for everyday wear, extraordinary in its making.
