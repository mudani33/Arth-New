Men's Hand-Printed Resort Shirt

$395

A relaxed resort shirt featuring artisanal hand block-printing by our craftspeople in Jaipur, India. Cut from lightweight organic cotton voile with a camp collar and relaxed fit — designed for warm-weather ease with the unmistakable character of handcraft. Each shirt's print is unique, bearing the subtle variations that only hand-printing can achieve.

Materials & Craft

100% organic cotton voile. Hand block-printed using traditional carved wooden blocks by artisan families in Jaipur, India. Natural, plant-based dyes create rich, nuanced color. Each print is applied by hand, stamp by stamp, making every shirt one-of-a-kind.

Coconut shell buttons. Camp collar with a straight hem designed to be worn untucked.

Size & Fit

Relaxed resort fit. Camp collar with a boxy, comfortable silhouette. Designed to be worn untucked. We recommend ordering your usual size for a relaxed look, or size down for a more fitted appearance.

Care

Machine wash cold on gentle cycle. Hang to dry or tumble dry low. The hand-printed pattern may soften and develop beautiful patina over time — this is a natural characteristic of artisanal block printing. Iron on medium heat if desired.

Shipping & Returns

Complimentary shipping on all orders. Made-to-order pieces ship within 4–6 weeks. Returns accepted within 14 days of delivery for unworn items in original condition.

The Craft

Printed by Hand,
Stamp by Stamp

Hand block-printing is one of India's oldest and most revered textile arts. In Jaipur, artisan families have carved wooden blocks and stamped fabric for centuries — a rhythmic, meditative process that produces patterns no machine can replicate.

Each Resort Shirt is printed using natural, plant-based dyes pressed onto organic cotton voile, stamp by stamp. The subtle imperfections — slight shifts in pattern, gentle variations in color — are not flaws but signatures of the human hand. No two shirts are exactly alike.
